d3-link-area

Links drawn as areas, for D3: the filled counterpart of d3.link. The edges are offset perpendicularly to the centerline, so the band keeps its visual thickness where it is steep, and it can change width from source to target.

Two offset Béziers vs. perpendicular offset

Drag the sliders. The black tick at the steepest point measures the thickness the eye actually reads.

Alluvial with different widths

Each flow leaves its source at one width and reaches its target at another (here the target column is rescaled), all as single closed paths.

Vertical

linkAreaVertical() swaps the axes.
